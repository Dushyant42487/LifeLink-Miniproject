LifeLink — An Intelligent Blood Donation, Inventory Management \& Demand Prediction Platform



LifeLink is a full-stack, AI-powered platform designed to connect blood donors, hospitals, and blood banks through a centralized system. It aims to simplify blood donation, improve blood inventory management, and support timely access to blood during medical emergencies.



The platform provides separate interfaces for three stakeholders: donors, hospitals, and blood banks, with role-based authentication and access control.



Key Features



Donor Management: Donor profiles, eligibility tracking, donation history, blood donation camp discovery, and emergency alerts.



Blood Inventory Management: Individual blood unit tracking, component-wise inventory management for red blood cells, plasma, and platelets, expiry monitoring, and testing status.



Hospital Services: Blood availability search across multiple centers, emergency and routine blood requests, patient sample details, and request tracking.



AI-Based Demand Prediction: Uses machine learning to forecast blood demand, identify potential shortages, and support better inventory planning.



Expiry and Wastage Prevention: Prioritizes blood units approaching expiry using the First Expiry, First Out (FEFO) approach, helping reduce avoidable wastage.



Secure Authentication: Role-based access for donors, hospitals, and blood banks.
